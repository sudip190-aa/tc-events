ABOUT TC EVENTS

Bringing Texas College Events Together.

TC Events is a college event platform designed to make discovering, registering, and experiencing events at Texas College simple and exciting.

OUR PLATFORM

One place for everything happening at Texas College.

From technology conferences and workshops to cultural programs, competitions, seminars, and student activities, TC Events helps students discover what's happening around campus.

Instead of relying on posters, social media posts, or word of mouth, students can find upcoming events, view complete event information, and register online.
